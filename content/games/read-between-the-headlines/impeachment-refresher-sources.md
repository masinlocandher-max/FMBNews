## Editorial guardrails

- Keep **alleged**, **according to testimony**, or equivalent attribution where a question concerns contested conduct.
- Do not convert witness statements into findings of guilt.
- Do not reveal the correct answer in wrong-answer feedback.
- Verify the latest completed trial day before replacing this weekly edition.
- Keep exactly 20 scored questions per edition.
