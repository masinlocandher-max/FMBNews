## Editorial guardrails

- Keep exactly 30 scored questions and 10 points per correct answer.
- At least 24 questions must come from the seven days immediately before the edition is released; no more than 6 may use directly relevant context from the previous 30 days.
- Randomize the question order and multiple-choice option order.
- Name and valid email are required before the scored run starts.
- Once a scored run begins, hiding the page, switching apps/tabs, refreshing, closing, or navigating away forfeits that attempt.
- Do not reveal the correct answer after a miss, skip, or timeout.
- Passing requires 21/30. The first run is the only points-earning run; failed players may make a zero-point qualification retry after the 24-hour cooldown.
- Verify each current-events bank again immediately before release and before reusing any source in a future edition.
