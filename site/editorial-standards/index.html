Editorial standards

FMB News exists to make important information easier to understand without weakening the evidence behind it.

Sources visible. Facts distinguished from claims. Corrections made openly.

Core standard

Clarity without shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Evidence before conclusion

    We prioritize primary documents, official records, direct statements, credible reporting, research, and data. A claim is not treated as established merely because it is repeated widely.

  2. 02

    Attribution stays visible

    Readers should be able to understand where material facts came from. When information originates with another newsroom, institution, researcher, witness, or document, that provenance should remain clear.

  3. 03

    Fact, analysis, and opinion stay distinct

    Verified facts are written as facts. Interpretation is identified as analysis. Opinion or commentary must not be presented as straight reporting.

  4. 04

    Uncertainty is explicit

    When evidence is incomplete, disputed, developing, or impossible to independently verify, the report should say so rather than manufacture certainty.

  5. 05

    Headlines must match the evidence

    Headlines, captions, thumbnails, and social copy should not overstate what the underlying reporting can support.

  6. 06

    Corrections are part of the record

    Material factual errors should be corrected clearly. Significant updates should preserve transparency about what changed and when.

  7. 07

    Commercial material is separated

    Sponsored, promotional, affiliate, or partner material must be distinguishable from independent editorial reporting. Payment or commercial relationships must not be disguised as neutral news judgment.

  8. 08

    Images require honest provenance

    Open-license and public-domain images retain source and license information where applicable. Supplied images are labeled as supplied when the creator or rights holder is not independently verified.

  9. 09

    AI may assist the process, not replace accountability

    Automated tools may assist with research organization, transcription, drafting support, comparison, or technical production. FMB News remains responsible for verification, attribution, editorial judgment, and the accuracy of what it publishes.
