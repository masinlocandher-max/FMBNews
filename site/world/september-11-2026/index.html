United States · Remembrance

Americans Mark 25 Years Since the September 11 Attacks

Families, officials and communities gathered in New York, at the Pentagon and in Shanksville to remember the nearly 3,000 people killed in the September 11, 2001 attacks. The New York ceremony again included the reading of victims’ names and moments of silence marking the attacks.

Why it matters

The attacks reshaped global security, aviation, U.S. foreign policy and public life far beyond the United States. Their political and human consequences remain visible a quarter-century later.

What changed this year

The commemoration included an additional moment of silence recognizing people who later died from illnesses associated with toxic exposure after the World Trade Center collapse.

India + Iran · BRICS + Maritime Security

Modi Presses Iran on Safe Navigation and Seafarer Protection Ahead of BRICS

Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi told Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian that freedom of navigation, commercial sea lanes and the safety of seafarers must be protected. The talks came on the eve of the BRICS leaders’ summit in New Delhi as attacks on vessels continued to affect regional trade.

Why it matters to Filipinos

Safe navigation in Gulf and Red Sea routes is directly relevant to Filipino seafarers and to Philippine fuel and freight costs. India’s emphasis also showed how maritime security had moved to the center of wider BRICS diplomacy.

What to watch

Whether BRICS could agree on language addressing the Iran conflict despite sharp differences among members and whether maritime attacks would ease.
