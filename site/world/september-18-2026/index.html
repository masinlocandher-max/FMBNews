Japan · Economy

Bank of Japan Raises Policy Rate to 1.25%, Highest in 31 Years

Verified facts

Reuters and AP independently reported that the BOJ raised its policy rate from 1.0% to 1.25% in a 7-2 vote, its highest level in 31 years.

Why it matters to Filipinos

Japan is a major Philippine trade, investment and development partner. Japanese rates can affect the yen, regional capital flows and financing conditions, although direct household effects are not predetermined.

What to watch next

BOJ guidance, Japanese inflation and wage data, the yen and spillovers into Asian financing conditions.
