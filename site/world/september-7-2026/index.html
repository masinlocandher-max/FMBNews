Germany · Politics

AfD’s Historic Saxony-Anhalt Victory Raises New Questions for Germany

The Alternative for Germany won a historic state-election victory in Saxony-Anhalt, sharply strengthening the far right and leaving migrant communities and political rivals confronting what the result could mean for immigration policy, coalition politics and Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s government.

Why it matters

The result is part of a wider European shift in which anti-immigration parties are gaining electoral power. Changes in Germany, the European Union’s largest economy, can influence EU migration, security and economic policy.

What to watch

Whether mainstream parties maintain their refusal to govern with the AfD, and whether the result changes national strategy before upcoming state and federal contests.

United Kingdom + United States · Ukraine

Burnham and Trump Say Work Toward a Russia-Ukraine Ceasefire Must Continue

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham and U.S. President Donald Trump discussed the war in Ukraine and agreed on the need to keep working toward a ceasefire, according to Downing Street.

Why it matters

The call came as U.S. envoys shuttled between Moscow and Kyiv. Diplomatic alignment among major Western governments is central to whether negotiations can produce a durable pause rather than another short-lived reduction in attacks.

What to watch

Any concrete ceasefire terms, security guarantees for Ukraine and evidence that Russia and Ukraine are prepared to move beyond exploratory diplomacy.
