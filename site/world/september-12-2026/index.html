Ukraine · War

Russian Attacks Kill Civilians Across Ukraine as Drone Barrage Intensifies

Russian attacks across several Ukrainian regions killed civilians, injured dozens and damaged homes and infrastructure. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said Russia had launched nearly 500 drones since early morning as strikes expanded beyond the front line.

Why it matters

The scale of the attacks increases pressure on Ukraine’s air defenses and civilian economy while diplomatic efforts remain unresolved.

What to watch

Further attacks on power, transport and industrial infrastructure, Ukraine’s retaliatory strikes inside Russia and additional air-defense commitments from allies.

Yemen · Red Sea Security

Houthi Advance Toward Bab el-Mandeb Raises Risk to a Second Major Oil Route

Houthi forces advanced south along Yemen’s Red Sea coast and reached Perim island in the middle of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The move increased the group’s ability to threaten another critical maritime chokepoint while the Strait of Hormuz was already heavily disrupted.

Why it matters to Filipinos

Bab el-Mandeb carries a significant share of global petroleum and trade. Disruption can affect shipping schedules, fuel prices and Filipino seafarers operating between Asia, the Middle East and Europe.

What to watch

Whether the Houthis try to restrict traffic, the scale of Saudi and Yemeni counterattacks and whether the United States becomes more directly involved.

Source

Reuters — Houthi advance toward Bab el-Mandeb
BRICS · Diplomacy

BRICS Adopts New Delhi Declaration Urging Maximum Restraint in Middle East

BRICS leaders unanimously adopted a joint declaration expressing deep concern over escalating Middle East tensions and calling for restraint, dialogue and diplomacy. Reaching consensus was notable because the expanded bloc includes both Iran and the United Arab Emirates.

Why it matters

The declaration tested whether an enlarged BRICS can act collectively on major geopolitical crises rather than function only as an economic grouping.

What to watch

How members translate the declaration into action, whether economic coordination deepens and how India-China relations develop after the summit.
