United States · Global Markets

U.S. 10-Year Treasury Yield Crosses 5% for the First Time Since 2023

The benchmark U.S. 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5%, its highest level since October 2023, as investors priced persistent inflation risk, higher oil prices, heavy debt issuance and a still-resilient U.S. economy.

Why it matters to the Philippines

Higher U.S. yields can strengthen the dollar, pull capital toward U.S. assets and raise financing costs across emerging markets. Those effects can add pressure to the peso and to public and private borrowing.

What to watch

The Federal Reserve’s next decision, oil prices, global bond-market spillovers and whether higher yields begin to materially weaken consumption and investment.

Iran + IAEA · Nuclear Diplomacy

Iran Protests After Nuclear Chief Is Prevented From Attending IAEA Conference

Iran protested after the United States opposed a sanctions exemption that would have allowed nuclear chief Mohammad Eslami to attend the International Atomic Energy Agency General Conference in Vienna. Austria had requested the exemption after initially granting a visa.

Why it matters

The dispute adds another layer of tension around Iran’s nuclear program at a time when military conflict and economic sanctions are already straining diplomacy.

What to watch

Whether the dispute affects cooperation with IAEA inspectors, the tone of the General Conference and prospects for renewed nuclear negotiations.
