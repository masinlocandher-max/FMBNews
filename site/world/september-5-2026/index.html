FMB Worldwide | September 5, 2026

U.S.-Iran military escalation reached crude-oil shipping while Washington’s envoys met Vladimir Putin in Moscow in a renewed attempt to move the Ukraine war toward negotiations.

United States + Iran · Security + Energy

U.S. Forces Strike Three Iranian Crude Carriers After Missile Attacks on Navy Ships

U.S. Central Command said American forces struck three Iranian crude-oil tankers after Iran launched ballistic missiles at two U.S. Navy ships. Central Command said no American personnel were harmed.

Why it matters to Filipinos

Military action involving oil shipping raises risks around Gulf energy supply, insurance and maritime traffic. Prolonged disruption can feed into Philippine fuel prices and affect Filipino seafarers working across the region.

What to watch

Further Iranian retaliation, restrictions around the Strait of Hormuz and whether attacks spread from military targets to a wider share of commercial shipping.

Source

Reuters — U.S. strike on Iranian crude carriers
Russia + United States · Ukraine Diplomacy

Putin Meets Witkoff and Kushner as Washington Revives Ukraine Peace Push

Russian President Vladimir Putin met U.S. special representatives Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in Moscow. Putin called continuing U.S.-Russia contacts beneficial, while the American delegation arrived seeking movement toward an end to the war in Ukraine.

What remains unclear

No public breakthrough or agreed settlement framework emerged from the meeting. The significance depended on whether Moscow and Kyiv would accept further direct or trilateral negotiations.

Why it matters

Any credible change in the war can affect European security, energy markets, food trade and the wider geopolitical environment in which the Philippines operates.

Source

Reuters — Putin meets U.S. envoys in Moscow
