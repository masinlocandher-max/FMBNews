Ukraine · War

Russia Hits Kyiv After Pause for U.S. Envoys’ Visit, Killing Five

Russia resumed drone and missile attacks on Kyiv after a brief halt around a U.S. diplomatic visit. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said five people were killed, while emergency officials reported dozens injured and damage to homes, a dormitory, a school, a clinic and other sites.

Why it matters

The attack underscored the gap between renewed diplomacy and conditions on the ground. Ukraine said ballistic missiles remained especially difficult to intercept and pressed allies for additional air-defense support.

What to watch

Whether U.S.-led talks produce any enforceable pause, and whether Russia’s use of faster drones and ballistic missiles changes Ukraine’s air-defense requirements.

Iran + Saudi Arabia · Regional Security

Iran Conflict Widens as Houthis Strike Saudi Cities and U.S. Hits More Iranian Tankers

The Middle East conflict escalated as Iran-aligned Houthis attacked four cities in southern Saudi Arabia, wounding dozens and setting oil installations ablaze. U.S. Central Command also said it destroyed five Iranian crude carriers, while Iran targeted a U.S. base in Jordan.

Why it matters to Filipinos

The widening conflict puts Gulf oil infrastructure, shipping routes and large overseas-worker communities under greater pressure. It also increases the risk of another fuel-price shock in the Philippines.

What to watch

Further attacks on Saudi energy facilities, Iranian restrictions on Gulf navigation and whether the confrontation pulls additional U.S. allies directly into the fighting.

Source

Reuters — Iran retaliation and Saudi attacks
