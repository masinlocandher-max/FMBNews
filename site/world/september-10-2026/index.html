Global Energy · Middle East

Oil Surges More Than 6% as Brent and U.S. Crude Move Above $100

Brent and U.S. crude climbed above $100 a barrel as attacks on tankers and worsening Middle East security raised fears of sustained supply disruption. Reuters reported Brent settling at $107.63 and West Texas Intermediate at $102.48.

Why it matters to Filipinos

The Philippines is a net oil importer. A prolonged move above $100 can affect pump prices, freight, electricity generation costs, food distribution and inflation.

What to watch

Shipping through Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb, Saudi energy infrastructure, OPEC supply and whether the price shock persists long enough to reach local consumer prices.

Source

Reuters — oil surge and tanker attacks
Eurozone · Monetary Policy

ECB Raises Policy Rate to 2.50% as Energy Shock Keeps Inflation Elevated

The European Central Bank raised its policy rate by a quarter point to 2.50%, its second increase of the year. ECB President Christine Lagarde said the Middle East conflict was continuing to generate inflation pressure while the economic outlook remained highly uncertain.

Why it matters

Higher European rates reinforce a broader global tightening cycle. For emerging economies, including the Philippines, stronger overseas yields can affect capital flows, currencies and borrowing costs.

What to watch

Whether oil remains above $100, how quickly inflation feeds into wages and prices, and whether the ECB signals additional increases.

Source

Reuters — ECB decision and Lagarde press conference
BRICS · India

India Prepares to Host BRICS as Iran War Tests the Expanded Bloc’s Unity

India prepared to host BRICS leaders in New Delhi with the U.S.-Iran conflict testing whether an expanded group containing both Iran and the United Arab Emirates could agree on a common position. The summit also put India-China relations and alternative financial systems in focus.

Why it matters

BRICS now represents a large share of the world’s population and output. Its decisions can influence trade, payments, technology standards and diplomatic alignment across the Global South.

What to watch

The summit’s language on the Middle East, proposals for cross-border payments and bilateral meetings involving India, China and Russia.
