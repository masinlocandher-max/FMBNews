Philippines · Politics

Prosecutors say they will drop the bribery article in the Duterte impeachment trial

Prosecutors say they will drop Article 3: House prosecutors said they will no longer pursue Article 3, the bribery allegation involving DepEd officials. Rep. Joel Chua confirmed the plan on Sunday. Lead prosecutor Rep. Gerville Luistro said on Monday that the panel would formalize its position before the Senate impeachment court on October 5. By Monday afternoon, no independent report had confirmed that the manifestation was on the record.

Background: The Supreme Court earlier dismissed three petitions against the Senate impeachment court's vote-threshold ruling, citing lack of standing and prematurity in reports dated September 30 and October 1. With 20 active senator-judges, reports put conviction at 14 votes. Vice President Sara Duterte has not been convicted of anything.

Why it matters

Dropping Article 3 leaves fewer allegations for the Senate to decide.

Next: whether the manifestation appears in the Senate record.

Philippines · Maritime

PCG and AFP report two West Philippine Sea incidents

Confirmed as Philippine agency statements: The PCG said Chinese coast guard vessels and a navy helicopter obstructed an operation against a suspected dynamite-fishing vessel near Pag-asa Island on October 4. The AFP said a Chinese J-16 repeatedly approached a Philippine C-208 patrol aircraft near Bajo de Masinloc on October 3. No injuries were reported.

No Chinese government account of either incident was available in Monday's reporting. The PCG says the China Coast Guard has no law enforcement authority in Pag-asa's territorial sea; China claims the area.

Why it matters

The PCG says dynamite fishing destroys the coral reefs and fish stocks that Filipino fishers depend on. The AFP said the J-16 maneuvers posed an aviation safety risk.

Philippines · Law enforcement

Zamboanga Sibugay raids

Confirmed with a count conflict: The Bureau of Immigration says 244 foreign nationals, most of them Chinese, were apprehended on October 2 at two suspected POGO sites in Siay and Alicia. The NBI director put the count at 234 on October 5.

Allegation, not proven: Those apprehended are being investigated for alleged immigration and POGO-related violations. The NBI is also looking at alleged trafficking and whether officials were involved. No charges or convictions have been reported.

Why it matters

POGOs are banned, and the NBI director says authorities are monitoring similar suspected sites elsewhere in Mindanao and the Visayas.

Next: a single headcount that both agencies agree on.

Philippines · Traffic

NCAP returns in five cities

Confirmed: Camera-based enforcement resumes today in Manila, Muntinlupa, Parañaque, Quezon City and San Juan. Valenzuela deferred its own restart because of roadworks. Quezon City's government page sets the same October 5 date.

Why it matters

Registered owners in those cities can get notices by SMS and email, and can check records on the "May Huli Ka" site and the eGovPH app, per Rappler.

Valenzuela had not reported a new start date by Monday afternoon.

Sources:FMB News: NCAP returnsInquirerRappler
Philippines · Economy

Fuel changes posted for Tuesday; inflation release also due

Company notices: Shell Pilipinas posted gasoline up ₱1.90 per liter, diesel down ₱1.30 and kerosene up ₱3.30, effective 6 a.m. on October 6, according to GMA News and Inquirer. Petro Gazz posted gasoline up ₱1.93 and diesel down ₱1.30. Other firms had not yet announced in those reports.

Forecast only: The PSA releases September inflation on Tuesday. BusinessWorld's poll of 22 analysts and the STAR's poll of 13 economists both have a median of 6.7%, against 6.1% in August. These are forecasts, not results.

Why it matters

Gasoline goes up at both firms on Tuesday, and diesel comes down by less than last week's rollback.

Tuesday's PSA release will show whether inflation came in near the 6.7% forecast median.

Sports · Asian Games

Philippines finishes with 29 medals

Confirmed: As the Games closed on Sunday in Aichi-Nagoya, Team Philippines had 29 medals: 4 gold, 9 silver and 16 bronze, and placed 17th, per SunStar and SPIN.ph. Carlos Yulo won two golds, EJ Obiena won pole vault with a Games record of 5.91 m and Jeus Yape won the fourth gold in virtual taekwondo.

Why it matters

The total is higher than the 18 medals in Hangzhou 2023 and 21 in Jakarta-Palembang 2018, per SPIN.ph.
