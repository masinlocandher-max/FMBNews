# FMB Crossword

Public model: weekly by category, never date-led.

The public crossword experience should present category choices and a category title. Publishing cadence may remain weekly internally, but dates should not be used as the puzzle's public identity.

Initial category architecture:
- Philippines
- World
- Politics
- Business
- Science & Technology
- Culture & Entertainment
- Sports

Each category can carry its own active puzzle and later its own previous-answer release without exposing a date as the primary label.
