Corrections policy

Accuracy matters more than defending an old version of a story.

When the record changes, the published record should change with it.

How corrections work

Material errors are corrected clearly.

  1. 01

    Report the issue

    Readers, sources, institutions, and subjects of coverage may send a correction request to withlovefmb@gmail.com. Include the story URL, the statement in question, and supporting evidence when available.

  2. 02

    Review the evidence

    FMB News compares the request with the original sourcing, available primary material, and any credible new evidence before deciding whether a correction or clarification is required.

  3. 03

    Correct material factual errors

    If a published statement is materially wrong, the story should be corrected. Significant corrections should be visible rather than silently changing the record.

  4. 04

    Clarify when wording can mislead

    A statement may be technically accurate but still require clarification if context, attribution, timing, or phrasing creates a materially misleading impression.

  5. 05

    Update developing stories transparently

    New information that changes the substance of a report should be reflected in the article's modified timestamp and, when appropriate, a visible update note.
