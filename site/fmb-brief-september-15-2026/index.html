Accountability · Impeachment

Sara Duterte’s SALNs move to the center of the trial.

Ombudsman records presented at the Senate showed Vice President Sara Duterte’s declared net worth rising to P98.66 million in 2025 while her filings listed no cash on hand or in banks from 2019 onward. Cross-examination also established that the prescribed SALN form has no separate line for cash and no column for acquisition costs of business interests.

Why it matters

The court must decide what the documents actually prove and how disclosure rules should be interpreted when the government’s own prescribed form leaves some information unspecified.

BARMM · Democracy

The first regular Bangsamoro parliamentary vote moves into coalition politics.

Roughly 80 percent turnout accompanied the September 14 election, and no single bloc emerged with an outright majority in the 80-seat parliament. Final seat allocation and coalition agreements will determine the next regional government.

Why it matters

This is the most important democratic handover yet under the Bangsamoro peace settlement: an appointed transition is giving way to an elected parliament that must build a governing majority.

Source:Reuters — BARMM coalition phase
Judiciary · Anti-graft

Romualdez stays in Payatas as the case moves toward arraignment.

Former House Speaker Martin Romualdez remained in the Quezon City Jail infirmary after the Sandiganbayan ordered his transfer from PGH. His arraignment was scheduled for September 16 in the plunder case filed by the Ombudsman. The allegations remain unproven and Romualdez maintains his innocence.

Source:Philippine News Agency — transfer to QC jail
Economy · Energy

Fuel, the peso and electricity supply squeeze at the same time.

Gasoline rose by P5.68 per liter, diesel by P4.31 and kerosene by P4.62. The peso touched P62.925 intraday before closing at P62.835, while the Visayas grid returned to red alert.

Why it matters

Higher imported-energy costs, currency weakness and constrained power supply can reinforce inflation pressure. The BIR’s removal of VAT from allowable system-loss charges trims one bill component, but does not erase the broader cost squeeze.

Sources:Philstar — fuel pricesPhilstar — system-loss VAT
What to watch next

Arraignment, conviction threshold, coalition talks and the cost of energy.

Watch the September 16 Romualdez arraignment, the Senate’s special session on the impeachment conviction threshold, final BARMM canvassing and coalition negotiations, the peso near the 63-per-dollar level, and whether Visayas generation capacity improves.

Today’s line

Process matters as much as the headline.

Courts, elections, disclosure systems and energy institutions are being judged by how they work under pressure.

Editorial note

Verified facts are separated from analysis. Allegations are attributed and are not presented as findings of guilt.
